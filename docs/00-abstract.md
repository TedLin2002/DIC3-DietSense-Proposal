## 中文摘要（約 400 字）

**A** 全球肥胖持續惡化：2024 年有 26 億名成人過重，其中 9.4 億人肥胖 [S1]；臺灣 18 歲以上成人過重或肥胖比例達 51.3% [S2]。**B** 然而，傳統飲食記錄 App 需手動搜尋、秤重並拆解複合料理，自我監測遵循率隨時間下降 [D3]；自報飲食約 30% 受試者低報 [D2]，全國調查錯報比例更超過 50% [D15]。通用 LLM 雖能理解自然語言，但以臺灣食品資料庫為參考時，熱量僅約六成落在 ±20% 內 [A2]，且易幻覺、無法追溯來源。**C** 本研究提出 DietSense，一個對話式、資料錨定且安全導向的飲食 Domain AI Agent。**D** 核心設計為「LLM 負責協調、領域工具負責計算、規則負責安全」：LLM 透過白名單 Function Calling 呼叫 USDA FoodData Central、臺灣食品營養成分資料庫與自建臺式料理知識庫（RAG），以確定性公式與 Mifflin–St Jeor TDEE 計算熱量並輸出低/中/高區間；資訊模糊時觸發最多兩輪的主動追問，並以獨立規則引擎攔截極低熱量與高風險語句。**E** 評估將以 500 筆臺式餐點標註資料集比較通用 LLM、手動記錄流程與移除追問的消融版本，指標包含熱量 MAE、±10%/±20% 達標率、區間涵蓋率、記錄時間、安全攔截率，並進行 20 人 × 7 天使用者研究（SUS）。**F** 預期 DietSense 能在 ±20% 內達成 75% 以上準確率（比通用 LLM 高至少 15 個百分點）、縮短記錄時間約 50%，並提供可追溯來源與不確定性區間（皆為預期目標，非實驗結果）。

## IEEE Abstract（English）

**Title—DietSense: A Data-Grounded and Safety-Aware Conversational AI Agent for Personalized Dietary Assessment and Weight Management**

**Abstract—** Overweight and obesity continue to rise worldwide, affecting an estimated 2.6 billion adults in 2024, including 940 million living with obesity [S1]; in Taiwan, 51.3% of adults are overweight or obese [S2]. Sustained dietary self-monitoring supports weight management, yet adherence declines over time [D3] because conventional trackers require manual food search, portion estimation, and decomposition of mixed dishes. Self-reported intake is also unreliable: roughly 30% of respondents under-report energy [D2], and doubly-labelled-water analyses indicate misreporting rates above 50% in national surveys [D15]. Although large language models (LLMs) enable natural-language interaction, their direct use for nutrient estimation yields unverifiable values—only about 58–61% of energy estimates fall within ±20% of Taiwanese food-composition references [A2]—and exposes users to hallucinated or unsafe advice [A5]. To address these limitations, this paper proposes DietSense, a data-grounded, safety-aware conversational AI agent. The agent adopts a tool-oriented architecture in which an LLM interprets intent and coordinates whitelisted function calls, while all numerical results are produced by deterministic services grounded in USDA FoodData Central, the Taiwan Food Nutrient Database, and a retrieval-augmented knowledge base of Taiwanese dishes. An eight-module pipeline covers speech input, intent classification, agent planning, data retrieval, nutrient analytics, domain-knowledge integration, recommendation, and interactive reporting. A bounded clarification loop resolves ambiguity in food identity and portion size, energy estimates are reported as low/central/high ranges, individual requirements are estimated with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, and an independent rule engine intercepts very-low-energy intake and high-risk language. The evaluation will use 500 annotated Taiwanese meal descriptions and compare DietSense against direct LLM estimation, a manual-logging workflow, and an ablation without clarification, measuring mean absolute error, within-±10%/±20% accuracy, interval coverage, logging time, and safety interception, complemented by a seven-day user study with 20 participants. DietSense is expected to deliver traceable, uncertainty-aware estimates with reduced logging burden; all numerical targets are stated as expected outcomes to be validated after implementation.

**Index Terms—** Conversational AI, domain-specific AI agents, dietary assessment, large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, tool use, uncertainty quantification, safety-aware systems, personalized weight management.
